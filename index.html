Synopsis

When India's Foreign Contribution Bill dies quietly on the floor of Parliament, nobody in the House calls it a defeat. Nothing was voted down. It was simply filed the way governments file things they intend to return to - not gone, only waiting.

Six thousand kilometres away, on the dry side of Mauritius, Ashwin is just another first-semester MBA student - pleasant, unremarkable, quietly falling for a classmate, Mrudula, who took her time warming to him. Neither of them has any reason to think twice about Aadhunik: a wealthy, short-tempered businessman already at war with his own government, whom Mrudula's parents have quietly decided would make her a fitting match.

Back in India, one NGO head after another takes a call from a stranger with a blurred face and a manipulated voice - and within the hour, their accounts are empty and their lives are over. The Mauritius police chase a tip that keeps leading nowhere. A food parcel keeps arriving, on schedule, at Ashwin's flat.

Two stories that were never supposed to touch are about to collide - and the man at the centre of both of them is not the one anyone in either country thinks he is.

Main Characters

Ashwin

An ordinary first-semester MBA student in Mauritius - easy to like, easy to overlook. He took the MBA, he says, for "a break from coding." Nobody around him has any reason to wonder what that means.

Mrudula

An Indian-Mauritian classmate, guarded at first and slow to say what she feels. Torn between the match her parents want for her and the one she's chosen for herself, she turns out to be the most decisive person in her own story.

Aadhunik

Wealthy, quick-tempered, and already estranged from his own government long before the story starts. Mrudula's parents think he's a fitting match for her. He has never once faced a consequence that stuck - not yet.
